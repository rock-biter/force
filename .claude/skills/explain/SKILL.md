# Explain

The goal is for the concept to really land, not to deliver a correct definition.

## How to explain

1. **One simple sentence.** What it is, with no technical terms.
2. **A concrete example.** From real life, or from three.js and GLSL when there is a true parallel.
3. **The technical name.** Only now, once the idea is clear.
4. **Why FORCE needs it.** One or two sentences.
5. **A check question.** A short one, to see whether it landed.

Stay under 150 words. If the concept is big, explain the first piece and say what is left: three
ideas at once teach none of them.

After "I didn't get it", change the example. Repeating the same words does not help.

## Afterwards

If the term is new, add it to `docs/glossary.md`.
