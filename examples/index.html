Demos

They run in a browser with WebGPU support.

  • heap-simulation: up to a million particles pushed apart on the GPU, live, and pushed away by the pointer
  • tilted-box: the same particles with friction, in a box that tilts by up to 30 degrees
  • cloth: a cloth of particles joined by springs, hanging by its top edge, pushed by the pointer
  • curtains: tall, narrow cloths hanging side by side, each its own color, grabbed and pulled with the pointer
  • cloth-pile: square cloths dropped in a heap on the floor, dragged out from under each other with the pointer
  • cloth-float: a cloth with no gravity, waving in slow currents, pulled with the pointer
  • ropes: ropes of particles joined by springs, hanging, swinging and pushed by the pointer
  • mixed-sizes: particles of different sizes, the largest up to four times the smallest, of one material or of several
  • debug-viewer: a small pile and the ways we draw it, with soft shadows, colors and the time a frame takes
  • compare: every way of pushing particles apart we studied, side by side, live
  • fountain: particles under gravity, in the debug viewer

The measurements and tests we run while we build, which print numbers rather than draw, are in the lab.

You can help: run the benchmark on your own device, a computer or a phone, and send us the numbers. It takes a couple of minutes.
