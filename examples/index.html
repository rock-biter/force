Demos

They run in a browser with WebGPU support.

Open the demos →

  • A heap of particles: up to a million particles pushed apart on the GPU, and pushed away by the pointer
  • A box that tilts: the same particles with friction, in a box that tilts by up to 30 degrees
  • Different sizes: particles of different sizes, the largest up to four times the smallest
  • Ropes: ropes of particles joined by springs: they hang, swing and are pushed by the pointer
  • A cloth: a cloth of particles joined by springs, hanging by its top edge
  • Curtains: tall, narrow cloths hanging side by side. Grab one and pull it
  • A pile of cloths: square cloths dropped in a heap. Drag one out from under the others
  • Cloths with no gravity: three cloths waving in slow currents, with no gravity. Grab one and pull
  • Shadows: a small pile and the ways we draw it, with soft shadows and colors
  • Side by side: every way of pushing particles apart we studied, running together
  • A fountain: particles under gravity, in the debug viewer

The measurements and tests we run while we build, which print numbers rather than draw, are in the lab.

You can help: run the benchmark on your own device, a computer or a phone, and send us the numbers. It takes a couple of minutes.
