Benchmark your device

FORCE is a physics engine that runs on the GPU. To choose how it works we measure, and so far on one machine only. This page runs the same measure on yours, a computer or a phone: how long its GPU takes to find which particles touch, in several ways, with up to 100 000 particles. It takes a few seconds on a recent computer, and maybe a minute or two on a phone. Keep the page open and in front while it runs.

Nothing is sent anywhere by this page. At the end you see the numbers, and you choose whether to send them to us as a public issue on GitHub. 



      

        
Your numbers

        
Milliseconds per step of 1/60 s spent on finding neighbours and pushing them apart. Lower is better.
