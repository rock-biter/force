Lab

The pages we use while we build FORCE. Most of them are not demos: they measure something, such as how long it takes to start a job on the GPU, or check that the GPU gives the same result as the same algorithm written in JavaScript. They print numbers rather than draw. Each one belongs to a chapter of the logbook, where those numbers are told and explained. They stay online as they were, so anyone can run them again on their own device.

They run in a browser with WebGPU support.

Measurements and tests

  • hello-compute: one compute shader, nothing else · logbook 000
  • falling-particles: the first world, printed as numbers and checked against JavaScript · logbook 008
  • pipeline-creation: creating a pipeline now or waiting for it, timed · guide ↗
  • dispatch-cost: what one more dispatch costs, timed · logbook 020, 022
  • round-trip: a trip from the GPU to JavaScript, timed · logbook 021, 022
  • turn-sorting: sorting contacts into turns in JavaScript, timed · logbook 023
  • contact-cost: finding and pushing apart the particles that touch, on the GPU, timed · logbook 030
