# 028 · A level strength of 1.5

*Accepted · November 23, 2026 · The user, making the value the live page already started with the default*

**Decision:** we will weigh the lower of two touching particles in a pile e^(1.5 × difference of levels) more by default: a level strength c of 1.5, not 2.

This supersedes the c = 2 of [decision 015](./015-half-and-half-on-positions.md). The rest of 015 stays: moving positions, half and half, masses seen as m^0.5, levels in the pile.

## Why

The live page and the tunable pane of the compare page have started with c = 1.5 since the user asked for a gentler start than 015, and that is what we have been looking at ever since. The default of FORCE+ said 2, and only the "as decision 015" button brought it back. Now the default is the value in use.

## Not chosen

- **c = 2, as 015:** not what the pages run with.

## Consequences

- One value everywhere: the live page, its reset button, and both FORCE+ panes of the compare page.
- The numbers of [chapter 029](../logbook/029-heavy-on-light.md), such as 1 kg sinking 0.2 mm under 100 kg, were measured with c = 2.

## Still open

- Not measured: how much more a heavy particle sinks into light ones with 1.5 than with 2.

## Revisit if

- Heavy particles sink visibly into light ones.
